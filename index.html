How to use: Expand a budget line to see phases, categories, and individual SOV tasks. Select a predecessor task by ID in the task’s Predecessors field; use commas for several IDs. Select from the suggestions or type an ID. “Apply predecessors” moves dependent tasks to begin the day after their latest predecessor finishes and keeps their duration. Summary dates and completion roll up automatically. Changes stay in this browser; use Save schedule to keep a portable copy, and Open saved schedule to resume it on another computer.
